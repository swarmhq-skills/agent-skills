# Screen time family agreement

A short agreement the whole family wrote together.

## Requirements

- The parent's answers to a few questions
- Optional: the child's own views

## When to use

- When rules need setting
- Start of a school term

## Steps

1. Ask when and where screens are fine and not fine in this home.
2. Ask what the child thinks is fair, if the parent wants to include them.
3. Draft a one-page agreement in plain words with a review date.
4. List two open questions for the family to talk through.

## Rules

- Use only what the parent says. Do not name the child in anything shared outside the family.
- Do not install or change settings without approval.
- Do not give medical or developmental claims. Point to the child's doctor or school for those.

## Output format

```
Family agreement (date)
- When and where
- What if rules break
- Review date
- Questions to discuss
```
