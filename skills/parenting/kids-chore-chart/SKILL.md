# Kids chore chart

A simple chart a child can follow on their own.

## Requirements

- The parent's list of chores
- The child's age range

## When to use

- At the start of a term
- When chores change

## Steps

1. Ask which chores the family wants shared and how old the children are, as a range.
2. Group chores by how often they happen.
3. Suggest a split that rotates fairly and fits the children's ages.
4. Draft a one-page chart with a column for each day.

## Rules

- Use only what the parent says. Do not name children in anything shared outside the family.
- Keep chores safe for the age given. Skip anything involving heat, tools or chemicals.
- Do not give child development advice. Point to a professional.

## Output format

```
Chore chart (week)
- Chore by day
- Who does it
- Rotation
- Review date
```
