# One-to-one prep

Walk into every one-to-one with a clear agenda.

## Requirements

- Previous one-to-one notes
- Calendar and recent threads

## When to use

- The day before a one-to-one

## Steps

1. Read the last notes and list the open actions and who owns each.
2. Scan recent threads with this person for items worth raising. Quote the source.
3. Draft an agenda: follow-ups, wins, blockers, one development topic.
4. Leave a space for the other person's own topics.

## Rules

- Read only. Do not send the agenda without approval.
- Keep notes about the other person private to the user.
- Do not judge performance. List facts and questions.

## Output format

```
Agenda (person, date)
- Open actions
- To raise: source
- Their topics
```
